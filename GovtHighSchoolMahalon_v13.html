Dear Parents & Guardians,

Your child arrives at school every morning carrying the weight of your dreams and the warmth of your love. As their teachers, we carry that responsibility with the deepest care.

This Parent-Teacher Meeting is our shared moment — a bridge between home and school. Together, we can see what your child is excelling at, understand where they need gentle guidance, and plan the path ahead as one united family committed to their future.

Your presence on 28 March 2026 is not just an attendance — it is a message to your child that their education matters deeply to you. That message is more powerful than anything I can ever say in a classroom.

I look forward to welcoming you, listening to you, and walking this journey together.

Brief Historyਸੰਖੇਪ ਇਤਿਹਾਸ

Government High School Mahalon has served the students of Mahalon village and surrounding areas of Tehsil Nawanshahr for many decades. Established under the Punjab School Education Board (PSEB), the school serves Classes 6th to 10th with UDISE Code 3060405702.
